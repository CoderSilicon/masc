# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

masc is a small, security-sensitive project. Everyone who participates — in issues, pull
requests, discussions, reviews, or private reports — should be able to do so without
harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.

We are committed to a respectful, focused, and technically honest environment. Disagreement
about code, design, or security is welcome. Personal attacks are not.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith unless there is evidence otherwise.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Give actionable feedback and explain the reasoning behind it.
- Be honest about uncertainty, limitations, and mistakes.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's payloads, filenames, passwords,
  identities, or private reports.
- Follow the [LICENSE](./LICENSE) and [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
- Accept that maintainers may decline a contribution or close a discussion to protect the
  project's scope, users, or legal position.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, stalking, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Discriminatory comments about a person's identity, background, or ability.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberate provocation, or sustained disruption.
- Personal insults, humiliation, or bad-faith accusations.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Encouraging or assisting unlawful use of masc, including hiding illegal material or
  violating another person's rights.
- Spamming, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or repeatedly reopening settled
  decisions.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including the repository, issues,
pull requests, reviews, discussions, and any private communication that relates to the
project. It also applies when you represent the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the
maintainers:

- Contact [@CoderSilicon](https://github.com/CoderSilicon) through GitHub.
- If the concern involves the repository owner, use GitHub's abuse-reporting tools.
- For a security vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) instead of this
  process. **Do not** include exploit details in a conduct report.

Please include what happened, where, when, who was involved, and any supporting context.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is
itself a violation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports and may take any action they consider proportionate,
including:

1. A private clarification or warning.
2. A request to edit or remove content.
3. Temporary limits on participation.
4. A permanent ban from project spaces.
5. Closing or reverting a contribution.
6. Referring the matter to GitHub or, where appropriate, law enforcement.

Enforcement decisions are made by CoderSilicon in its sole discretion. Maintainers will
avoid conflicts of interest and will not act as judge in a dispute where they are directly
involved if another reasonable path exists.

## Good-faith security research

Security research conducted under [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) is not a Code of Conduct
violation. Coordinated, private disclosure is expected. Public disclosure before a fix is
available may be treated as a violation.
